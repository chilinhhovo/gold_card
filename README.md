# The Most Price-Inflated Item In America
The $5 Million Price Tag on the American Dream

View project here: https://chilinhhovo.github.io/gold_card/

# Goal 
- To explore the real-world implications of Trump’s proposed ‘Gold Card’ visa, which prices U.S. residency at $5 million
- Contextualize this dramatic 900% price increase from 2017's EB-5 visa within the broader American economic landscape
- Translate complex, abstract policy into everyday terms—eggs, burgers, Birkin bags, and retirement years
- Ask deeper questions about wealth, access, and whether U.S. residency is becoming just another luxury good

# Final Output
- Illustrated explainer webpage
- Comparative visualizations (what $5M can buy in eggs, Big Macs, condos, cars)
- Retirement cycle map showing how long $5M could last in different parts of the U.S.
- Policy breakdown with an editorial storytelling tone

# Design Stack
HTML + CSS
Illustrator
Photoshop
Datawrapper (for map + charts)
Google Sheets (for calculations + data tracking)

# Key Takeaways 
The price hike from $500K (2017) to $5M (2025) is unprecedented, signaling a sharp pivot toward ultra-wealthy immigrants
In practical terms, $5M could buy 413 Birkin bags, 2,800+ cookies a day, or 10 years of retirement across many U.S. states
While luxury comparisons are eye-catching, the deeper story is about who’s excluded as residency becomes commodified
This project is part data explainer, part cultural critique
